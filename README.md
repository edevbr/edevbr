## What happened to my previous account

My previous GitHub account, evandrodevbr, was suspended after one of my repositories was flagged. That repository was my mistake, made without bad intent, and I offered to delete it right away so it could not affect anyone.

The offer changed nothing. There was no way to remove that one repository and keep the account, and the suspension made no distinction between it and years of unrelated work. I lost my personal repositories and the ones I maintained for companies I worked for, along with their issues, reviews and history.

I had backups, so the code survived. Since then my development setup runs on my own machines and my own server, where a decision I have no say in cannot take years of work offline.
